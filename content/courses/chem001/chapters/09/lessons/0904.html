Chapter Summary: Introduction to Organic Chemistry

Chapter Big Idea

Organic chemistry studies carbon-containing compounds and explains how their structures determine their properties and applications. Carbon's ability to form chains, rings, and numerous functional groups makes it the foundation of millions of compounds used in everyday life.

1. Organic Chemistry at a Glance

Topic Key Idea
Organic Chemistry Study of compounds primarily containing carbon and hydrogen.
Carbon Forms four covalent bonds and exhibits catenation.
Organic Compounds Generally contain C—H bonds.
Inorganic Compounds Usually do not contain C—H bonds.

2. Organic vs Inorganic Compounds

Property Organic Inorganic

Examples Glucose, Ethanol, Propane NaCl, H₂O, NH₃

Comprehensive Practice Set

1. Classify each compound as organic or inorganic: CH₄, NaCl, CO₂, C₂H₅OH.

Organic: CH₄, C₂H₅OH
Inorganic: NaCl, CO₂

2. Which hydrocarbon family does C₂H₄ belong to?

Alkene

3. Which hydrocarbon family contains only single bonds?

Alkanes

4. Which functional group is present in ethanol?

Alcohol (–OH)

5. Which functional group is responsible for the sour taste of vinegar?

Carboxylic acid (–COOH)

6. Which functional group commonly produces fruity odors?

Esters

7. Which functional group is found in proteins?

Amides

8. Identify the hydrocarbon family of benzene.

Aromatic hydrocarbon

9. Explain why hydrocarbons are generally insoluble in water.

Hydrocarbons are nonpolar while water is polar.

10. Identify the functional group in CH₃COCH₃.

Ketone

11. Which compound is an aromatic hydrocarbon: propane, benzene, ethene, or ethyne?

Benzene

12. Why is carbon capable of forming millions of compounds?

Because carbon forms four covalent bonds and exhibits catenation.

13. Which functional group is found in acetone?

Ketone

14. Explain one environmental concern associated with hydrocarbon combustion.

Burning hydrocarbons releases carbon dioxide, contributing to climate change. Incomplete combustion may also produce carbon monoxide.

15. A chemist discovers a compound containing a benzene ring and an –OH group. Which hydrocarbon class and functional group are present?

Hydrocarbon class: Aromatic
Functional group: Alcohol (hydroxyl)

Chapter Summary

  • Organic chemistry focuses on carbon-containing compounds and their structures.
  • Carbon's ability to form four covalent bonds allows the formation of millions of compounds.
  • Hydrocarbons are classified as alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, and aromatic hydrocarbons.
  • Functional groups determine the chemical and physical properties of organic compounds.
  • Recognizing functional groups allows scientists to predict the behavior and applications of organic molecules.
  • Organic compounds play essential roles in fuels, medicines, plastics, agriculture, food, and countless engineering applications.
